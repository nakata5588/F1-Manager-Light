# Historical Environment Tracks

These assets are presentation layers only. They may contain scenery, water, buildings, kerbs and visual start/finish markings. They are not physical race geometry.

The functional centerline and track metadata control car movement, sector boundaries, grid placement, incident placement and pit-lane logic. The Race Engine remains authoritative for sporting outcomes.

## Buenos Aires 1980

`buenos-aires-no15-1980.svg` represents Circuit No. 15. Its upper-straight S/F and rightward race direction are mirrored by the independent verified centerline in `src/data/historicalTrackGeometry.js`.
